01

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide analytical information to website operators.

Cookies are set either by the website you are visiting or by third party services embedded on that site. They may be stored for the duration of your browser session or for a defined period after you leave the site.

Related technologies

In addition to cookies, StephensLab may use the following similar technologies:

  • Local Storage: Browser based storage used to save your cookie preferences. Data is stored on your device and not transmitted to our servers.
  • Session Storage: Temporary storage cleared automatically when you close your browser tab. Used for transient UI state only.
  • HTTP Headers: Certain preference signals such as Do Not Track (DNT) are read from your browser headers and respected by our platform.
02

How We Use Cookies

StephensLab uses cookies strictly and purposefully. We follow a data minimization approach. We only deploy cookies that are necessary for a stated, legitimate purpose. We use cookies for the following broad purposes:

  • Site security: CSRF tokens and session identifiers to protect forms and authenticated areas.
  • Functionality: Remembering preferences such as display mode, language, or newsletter dismissal state.
  • Performance measurement: Understanding which articles are most read, how long visitors engage, and where they navigate from. We use anonymized, aggregated data only.
  • Compliance: Recording your cookie consent choices so we can demonstrate compliance with GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive.

We do not use cookies for behavioral advertising, cross site tracking, or profiling of individual users.

04

Third Party Cookies

StephensLab is designed to minimize third party dependencies. At present, we do not embed third party services that set cookies on your device. The following external resources are loaded as static assets and do not set cookies:

  • Google Fonts: Loaded as a CSS stylesheet for typography. Google may log the request IP address, but no cookies are set by this request. Refer to Google's privacy policy for their data handling practices.
  • CDN hosted CSS and JS: Open source libraries loaded from public CDNs. These are static asset requests and do not set cookies or track users.

Should StephensLab integrate any third party service that sets cookies in the future, such as embedded video players, comment systems, or social sharing buttons, this page will be updated and consent will be sought before deployment.

05

Your Preferences

You are in full control of your cookie preferences on StephensLab. Your rights under GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive include:

  • Right to withdraw consent: You may withdraw consent for any optional cookie category at any time by returning to this page and updating the toggles above. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.
  • Right to refuse: Refusing optional cookies will not prevent you from accessing any content on StephensLab. No content is gated behind cookie consent.
  • Right to information: You are entitled to clear information about every cookie we use, which is provided in the full cookie list in Section 7.
  • Do Not Track: StephensLab respects the browser level Do Not Track (DNT) signal. If DNT is enabled, analytics and preference cookies will not be loaded regardless of your saved preferences on this page.
06

Managing Cookies via Your Browser

In addition to the preference controls on this page, you can manage, block, or delete cookies at any time using your browser settings. Instructions for the most common browsers are available at the following links:

  • Google Chrome: support.google.com/chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox: support.mozilla.org
  • Apple Safari: support.apple.com/safari
  • Microsoft Edge: support.microsoft.com/edge

Important note

Blocking all cookies through your browser settings, including essential cookies, may cause parts of StephensLab to behave unexpectedly. In particular, CSRF protection on forms requires the csrftoken cookie to be present. Blocking it may prevent form submissions from working correctly.

Cookie Retention

Cookies are retained for the durations specified in the cookie list above. At the end of a cookie's retention period, the cookie is automatically deleted by your browser. You may also delete cookies at any time manually through your browser settings or by using the "Reject all optional" button above.

Your consent preferences stored in sl_consent are retained for 12 months. After this period, we will prompt you to review and re-confirm your preferences to ensure they remain current and accurate.

Session cookies are deleted automatically when you close your browser. They store no personally identifiable information and carry no data across sessions.
