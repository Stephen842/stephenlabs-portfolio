StephensLab Promise

Our mission is to provide high quality technical education while protecting your privacy. To earn and maintain your trust, we commit to transparency, security, and data minimization. We keep your personal information personal and private. We will not sell or rent your personal information. We only collect what is necessary to deliver quality content and operate our email infrastructure.

This StephensLab Privacy Policy ("Policy") outlines the personal information that StephensLab ("we", "us", "our", or "StephensLab") gathers, how we use that personal information, and the options you have to access, correct, or delete such personal information. StephensLab is operated by Ugota Chisom Stephen, Data Controller based in Germany.

01

Policy Application

This Policy applies to StephensLab collection, use, and disclosure of personal information from the following categories of data subjects:

  • Newsletter Subscribers: Individuals who subscribe to receive email communications from StephensLab.
  • Website Visitors: Those who visit our Websites, including those who may opt to provide contact information, fill out a form, or provide feedback.
  • Contact Form Users: Individuals who reach out to us via contact forms or email inquiries.

StephensLab Website and Services are not intended for, nor designed to attract, individuals under the age of thirteen (13) without parental consent. However, our content is family friendly and appropriate for young learners interested in technology.

02

Information We Collect

Newsletter Subscribers

Email address: When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. We operate our own email infrastructure. Your data never passes through third party email marketing platforms.

Website Visitors

Log files: When you visit our Websites, we gather certain information and store it in log files. This information may include IP addresses (anonymized where possible), browser type, system configuration, referring pages, and locale preferences.

Cookies: We may use minimal cookies for essential site functionality and analytics. You may control cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Contact Form Users

Contact information: When you submit a message via our contact form, we collect your email address, name (if provided), and any information you choose to include in your message.

What we do not collect

We do not collect passwords (no user accounts exist), payment information (all content is free), government IDs, biometric data, precise GPS location, or third party tracking data.

03

How We Use Information We Collect

StephensLab only processes personal information in a way that is compatible with and relevant to the purpose for which it was collected or authorized. We may use your personal information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Websites for all users
  • Send transactional messages, including responses to your comments, questions, and requests
  • Provide customer service, support, and technical notices
  • Send commercial communications in accordance with your preferences (you may unsubscribe at any time)
  • Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities in connection with the Websites
  • Comply with legal obligations as well as to investigate and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information for marketing or advertising purposes.

04

Data Aggregation

StephensLab may aggregate non-personally identifiable data about our visitors and subscribers. For example, we may compile statistics on article popularity, reading time, or geographic distribution of readers. Aggregated data does not identify any individual and may be used to improve our content.

05

Information Sharing

We work with service providers who help us operate our business and deliver the StephensLab experience. These may include hosting providers and infrastructure services. These service providers may only process personal information pursuant to our instructions and in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

Specifically, we do not permit our service providers to sell any personal information we share with them or to use it for their own marketing purposes.

In addition, we may share your information in the following circumstances: to comply with legal process, to protect our rights or property, in the event of a business transfer (with notice), or as you may otherwise consent.

06

Notice to EU, UK and Swiss Residents

"Personal information" as referenced in this Privacy Policy means "personal data" as defined under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and its UK counterpart. StephensLab is a data controller for the personal information collected from all categories of data subjects listed above.

If you are an individual from the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, our legal basis for collecting and using your personal information will depend on the information collected and the specific context. We normally collect personal information only where we have your consent, where we need it to perform a contract, or where the processing is in our legitimate interests.

07

International Information Transfers

StephensLab is operated from Germany but serves a global audience. We primarily store your information on servers located within the European Economic Area (EEA). To facilitate our global operations, we may transfer and access information from other countries in compliance with applicable laws.

Whenever we transfer personal information originating from one country to another, we implement appropriate safeguards, including EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and Data Processing Agreements (DPAs), consistent with the laws of the territory from which the data is exported.

08

Data Subject Rights and Choices

StephensLab recognizes individuals' data protection rights. Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, update, port, or delete your personal information, and to restrict or object to processing (each a "Rights Request").

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at ugotachisomstephen@gmail.com with "Privacy Request GDPR" in the subject line. We will respond within 30 days as required by Art. 12(3) GDPR.

Please note that before we can complete your Rights Request, we will need to verify your identity to ensure we are responding to the correct individual. You can expect a verification email to the address associated with your request.

09

Communication Preferences

StephensLab will send you commercial communications based on your subscription preferences. You may manage your receipt of commercial communications by clicking the "unsubscribe" link located at the bottom of any marketing email, or by contacting us directly. Even if you unsubscribe, we may still send you transactional messages related to your inquiries or requests.

10

Data Security, Data Integrity, and Access

We take all reasonable steps to protect information we receive from you from accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, and unauthorized disclosure or access. We have put in place appropriate physical, technical, and administrative measures to safeguard your information, including TLS/SSL encryption, access controls, and regular security assessments.

11

Data Retention

We store your personal information for a period of time that is consistent with the business purposes set forth in this policy or as long as needed to fulfill legal obligations. The criteria we use to determine retention periods include:

  • Purpose of collection: Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe.
  • Legal requirements: German tax law may require retention of certain records for up to 10 years.
  • Business necessity: Contact form inquiries are retained for up to 12 months after resolution.

When the data retention period expires, we will delete or destroy the information. If for technical reasons we are unable to do so, we will implement appropriate security measures to prevent further use.

12

Notification of Changes

If we make changes to this Policy that we believe materially impact the privacy of your personal information, we will promptly provide notice of any such changes (and, where necessary, obtain consent), as well as post the updated Policy on this website noting the effective date of any changes.

13

Business Transactions

We may assign or transfer this Policy, as well as information covered by this Policy, in the event of a merger, sale, change in control, or reorganization of all or part of our business. You will be notified of any such transfer.

14

Notice to California Residents (CCPA)

This section applies only to California residents. It describes how we collect, use, and share Personal Information of California residents and their rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

Your California Privacy Rights: You have the right to know what personal information we have collected about you, request deletion of your personal information, opt out of the sale of your personal information (we do not sell data), and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us at ugotachisomstephen@gmail.com with "CCPA Request" in the subject line.

15

Children's Privacy

StephensLab is a family friendly, youth inclusive platform. Our content contains zero explicit material and is designed to inspire young enthusiasts passionate about computing, programming, and technology.

However, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you are under 16, please obtain parental consent before subscribing to our newsletter. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.
